Account recovery

You came in with your backup email. To finish, set up a new Monero wallet (Cake: create a new one) and switch this account to it below. After that you sign in with the new wallet as usual.

Linked wallets

We never store your full address or your email, only a short label so you can recognise them. You type them in when they're needed.

    New Monero wallet

    Lost your device or wallet? Recover with your backup email and recovery code (on the sign-in page), set up a new wallet (Cake: create a new one), and switch this account to its address. Your old address stops working for sign-in. This moves your account only: coins in a lost wallet can only come back from its seed phrase.

    Sign this in the NEW wallet (Cake: Settings → Other → Sign / Verify), then paste the signature.

    Backup email + recovery code (optional)

    For recovery only, if you lose your Monero wallet. You still sign in with Monero. Recovery needs both your backup email (we send a code) and your recovery code, which you write down once. Someone who gets into your inbox, a hacker or anyone else, can't take your account without the recovery code. One email can back up several accounts. Never shown publicly, never used for newsletters.

    With a waiting period, if someone ever starts a recovery, signing in with your Monero wallet during the wait cancels it.

    Your recovery code: save it now

    Letters only (no I or O). Write it down or print it and keep it offline (not in the same email inbox). It's shown only this once; we keep only a fingerprint of it. Lost it? Make a new one here while you can still sign in.

    Your recovery code is what you'll need, together with this email, to get back in if you lose your Monero wallet (sign-in page → "Lost your Monero wallet?"). Don't have it written down? Make one now, while you can still sign in.

    Delete my account

    Removes your account, its wallet link, backup email and recovery code. It can't be undone. You can sign up again later with the same wallet as a brand-new account. For your safety you'll also confirm with your wallet.
