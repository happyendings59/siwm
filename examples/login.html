Sign in

No email, no password. You prove it's you by signing a short message in your wallet. Nothing is sent or spent.

1. Copy this message

2. Sign it in your wallet

Cake Wallet: Settings → Other → Sign / Verify, paste the message, tap Sign Message, then copy the signature (starts with SigV2). Use a regular wallet; hardware-wallet Monero can't sign messages in Cake.

The code works once and expires in 10 minutes.

No Monero wallet yet?

Cake Wallet is free and can sign the message. On a phone, sign in from the phone's browser so you can copy and paste between the two apps.
